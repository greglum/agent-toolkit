## Primer

### How it works

- The cooperative grades each cocoon lot by reeling a sample under LSG-4. [S2 §3.1; Checked]
- Farms are paid within two weeks of grading.

### Entities and processes

- Each batch gets one intake record. [S1 §2.1; Checked]

### Metrics and traps

- Mean reel length of the sample sets the grade. [S2; Checked]

### Newcomer mistakes

- Newcomers call a cocoon lot a `batch`. [S1 §2.4; Checked]
