What this model explains

This is an ideal planar linkage with illustrative dimensions: a 40 mm crank radius and a 120 mm rod. It calculates position, with rigid links, frictionless joints and no offset between the crank centre and slider guide. It does not calculate force, pressure, speed or the behavior of a particular machine.

Equations and checks

For crank angle θ, radius r and rod length L, the crank pin is (r cos θ, r sin θ). The slider lies at x = r cos θ + √(L² − r² sin² θ). Displacement is r + L − x. The rod uses the right-hand assembly branch.

At 0° the slider is 160 mm from the fixed joint. At 180° it is 80 mm away. At 90° it is approximately 113.137 mm away. These are analytical checks of an illustrative model, not measurements. The model covers 0° to 360° and requires L > r > 0.
