## Trend references, October 25, 2026

[Virlo Content Trends](https://dev.virlo.ai/docs/content-trends) separates reusable
formats from topics, defines stages from creator counts, and documents discovery
lag and partial recent days. This informs the conditional source-note procedure,
not a vendor selection or a forecast. [YouTube Trends](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757?hl=en)
uses audience/search context and notes coverage limits. Neither establishes that
an outside format will serve a particular campaign. Keep population, units and
observation separate from interpretation. No API, schema field or collector added.
The catalog source index records the dated assessment, source coverage and limits.
