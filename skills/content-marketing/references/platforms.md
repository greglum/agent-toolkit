# Platform and traffic boundaries

Checked October 26, 2026. Recheck these living sources when a platform, account entitlement
or reporting definition changes. This is a local-file workflow, not an account integration.

## YouTube

[Sharing links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en-GB): Shorts description
and comment URLs are not clickable. Use a verified related-video or channel-profile route
instead. Long-form external links need the account's advanced-feature access; check the
actual account before marking a placement verified. Don't promise a clickable link from a
draft alone. A [Shorts related video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)
must be on the same channel and public or unlisted. Its guide says advanced-feature access
is required, while the [feature table](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9890437?hl=en)
also lists phone verification. Check actual Related Video availability in the channel's
Short editor before relying on that route; the documents alone do not settle entitlement.

For a series, distinguish batch preparation from public release. YouTube
[limits notifications](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7389684?hl=en) to three per
channel per viewer in 24 hours; publishing more than three videos in a short period may
halt notifications for 24 hours even if most were opted out. Private uploads can be prepared
together and published separately. This is a notification limit, not a three-video upload cap.

The [performance FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en) establishes no
daily or weekly quota and no known long-term performance effect from publication time.
Choose spacing for audience attention, production capacity, link dependencies and time to
learn; do not promise an algorithm benefit. The [schedule guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13616979?hl=en)
supports sustainable frequency and avoiding audience overload, not a universal interval.

[Advanced Mode comparisons](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16766491?hl=en) support
comparing the same elapsed publication lifespan. Use a filtered export or genuine snapshots;
do not apply a universal view-growth curve to infer missing observations. Match formats.

## LinkedIn

[Personal post analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6538517) distinguish
impressions from visits to external links, including repeat visits. Definitions and available
metrics vary by content and account. [Page analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564051)
use broader click definitions. Never relabel generic clicks as website traffic or video watches.
Keep paid and organic results in separate scopes. Aggregate exports suffice; no viewer
demographics or identity inference is needed for this campaign loop.

Do not prescribe a posting hour, link-in-comments rule, hashtag recipe or reach advantage
without task-specific evidence. Choose the angle and CTA for the audience and measure it.

## Owned website

[Campaign parameters](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en) identify
referrals when the destination's analytics captures them. The builder uses source, medium,
campaign and content tags consistently. It neither installs analytics nor proves capture.
Use the existing authorized reporting system. If it cannot report campaign-tagged sessions,
say attribution is unavailable and use an actually supported metric, such as platform outbound
clicks. Those clicks are not automatically sessions or unique people.

Keep internal website links untagged so the link plan does not overwrite acquisition context.
Keep links to YouTube and LinkedIn canonical; a query tag there is not our website attribution.
