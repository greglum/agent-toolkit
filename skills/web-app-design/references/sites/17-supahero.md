# 17. Supahero

Observed: 2026-10-05. Depth: targeted web search, public homepage, Linear hero entry, and Supahero pricing page. All sampled pages were readable without login. Images were identified in page content; original sites were not exercised. No accessibility, conversion, or performance measurements were made.

Research method: searched `site:supahero.io hero sections design`, opened the official site, and followed the primary pages listed below.

## Purpose and useful evidence

[Supahero](https://supahero.io/) is a curated website hero-section library. It now announces that it is part of [screensdesign.com](https://screensdesign.com/). This is a material change from treating it as an independent, fixed catalog. The announcement links to a UX app library, but that adjacent product was not comprehensively reviewed in this page's scope.

The homepage lists references from product companies, portfolios, studios, retail, and other sectors. The sampled [Linear entry](https://supahero.io/hero/linear) provides a hero image, a brief product description, an October 30, 2024 date, and an original-site link. That makes it useful for locating a visual reference, but the old capture date cannot establish Linear's current hero or application design.

The [pricing page](https://supahero.io/pricing) sells promoted placement and newsletter exposure. The observed prices concern advertising slots, not a browsing subscription. Promised impressions and clicks are vendor marketing estimates. Featured positions may therefore reflect paid visibility as well as curation; they should not be read as independent rankings of usability.

## Patterns and application fit

Use hero references to examine the order of headline, explanation, product evidence, and primary action. For a public web app page, the strongest practical question is whether a visitor can understand the product and its next step without decoding an abstract animation. A product screenshot may explain an unfamiliar tool better than a purely decorative shape.

In a working application, a landing-page hero rarely transfers intact. Oversized headlines, large empty areas, and dramatic image treatments can reduce usable space for a user who already knows the product. Apply the reference to onboarding, a feature overview, or a public entry point only when that matches the task. Daily dashboards need data and actions arranged for repeated use.

Choose references with comparable content volume and product complexity. Write down the visual hierarchy and compositional idea, then remake that structure with our real content and tokens. Do not request a clone of Linear or another recognizable brand. A still image is an incomplete reference for scroll behavior, responsive layout, or transitions.

## Reuse, access, and integration

The sampled pages do not provide a verified license for reusing reference screenshots, brand imagery, copy, fonts, or original source code. The footer reserves rights. No downloadable implementation package was verified for the sampled hero. The site's Tools and Templates links are separate resources and should be checked individually before use. Browsing alone adds no frontend dependency and requires no purchase in this sample.

## Skill use and checks

Route public hero composition here. The agent should create an original content-first composition, record its chosen reference and capture date, and explain the transferable idea. Keep source imagery out of the skill repository unless explicit rights exist.

Analytical checks: headline wrapping on narrow screens, readable image text, meaningful alt text, stable image dimensions, loading cost, keyboard access to the CTA, and reduced motion for decorative effects. Do not assume a curated hero is fast or accessible because it looks polished.

Recheck when Supahero completes its migration, paid placement affects a chosen reference, or a selected site's current layout or reuse rights matter.
