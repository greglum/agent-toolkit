## Useful patterns and fit

For a working app, illustration is most useful when it helps an empty state, onboarding explanation, learning flow, or a small product story. Pair the image with a useful heading and the next action. A decorative tree or object cannot explain why a user's records are missing or replace a clear recovery option. For a marketing page, a consistent kit can provide a distinctive visual language for explanatory scenes or feature storytelling.

Those recommendations are design analysis. The collection was not tested for recognizability, cultural suitability, contrast, accessibility, or conversion. A future selection should inspect the actual artwork and its emotional tone. A playful dungeon object may suit a game community and distract from a financial approval workflow. Modern appearance comes from a coherent choice, not the sheer asset count.
