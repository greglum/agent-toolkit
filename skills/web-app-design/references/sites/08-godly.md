## Useful patterns and working method

Use this resource for visual range when the starting point feels generic. Compare typography scale, composition, image treatment, accent placement, and how a brand establishes a recognizable voice. Its broader feed can expose alternatives to the familiar gradient-card look, but the skill should explain the relationship between each visual idea and our product.

The [website collection](https://recent.design/websites) includes SaaS, finance, product, technology, and other category filters. Use those to narrow the comparison set. A visually arresting artist portfolio and a repeat-use analytics workspace have different priorities. Borrow useful hierarchy or texture without importing a page's entire atmosphere into a task that demands concentration.

The [app screenshot collection](https://recent.design/app-store-screenshots) is explicitly about app-store presentation. It should not be treated as a library of verified in-app journeys. Promotional composites, captions, and carefully selected screens can suggest type and layout ideas, but they do not establish input, error, loading, or navigation behavior.

Suggested workflow:

1. Define whether the task is brand expression, public product explanation, or repeated app work.
2. Pick the corresponding collection and identify one or two relevant references.
3. Record what the reference communicates through visual hierarchy in original words.
4. Find the original creator/product source before relying on behavior or asset rights.
5. Translate the idea into the existing system and validate realistic content and states.

## Access, source, and reuse

Public browsing worked without an account or payment in this review. Sponsor links and design job listings were visible. No verified code-download license, image reuse license, API, or MCP contract was found in the inspected navigation or targeted legal searches. Treat reuse rights as **unknown**. The feed's presence and promotional imagery do not confer permission to copy original brands, photographs, app screens, or illustrations.

Keep documentation to direct URLs and original analysis. Do not store a mirror of the image feed or import remote assets into an app. Any chosen code or asset needs its own first-party provenance and permission.
