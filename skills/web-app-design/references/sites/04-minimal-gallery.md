## What it offers

Minimal Gallery curates websites, templates, and creative tools for visual research. Its about page describes a gallery running since 2013 and notes acquisition in 2026. Its taxonomy includes app, SaaS, software, finance, healthcare, ecommerce, portfolio, and animation. This makes it useful for finding a relevant comparison set rather than treating every polished site as equivalent. [About](https://minimal.gallery/about/), [Catalog and categories](https://minimal.gallery/)

An inspected [Unify entry](https://minimal.gallery/unify/) includes the original site URL, creator credits, publication date, and desktop/mobile view controls. An entry provides a useful starting point and provenance; it is not a usability report or a complete interaction specification.

## Useful patterns and working method

Use this gallery when a page lacks visual coherence: weak typography, uncertain spacing rhythm, indistinct sections, or imagery that does not support the content. A minimal reference can help identify how much structure is possible with alignment, scale, and selective emphasis rather than adding more cards or effects.

Choose two or three references from the relevant product category. For each, note the reading path, the number of competing actions, the relationship between headings and body copy, and how much information appears before scrolling. The skill should record what each reference solves in our context. “Looks premium” is too vague to implement or verify.

For marketing pages, the gallery is a strong source of composition and brand pacing. For working apps, use app/software categories to study shell and visual hierarchy, then inspect actual task flows elsewhere. A beautiful public homepage reveals little about editing, validation, authorization, long tables, or recovery from errors. Do not let empty marketing space dictate an operational screen's density.

After selecting a reference, follow its original site and inspect the needed breakpoint and interaction. Screenshot crops can omit sticky elements, hidden navigation, content below the fold, or the moment a layout becomes difficult. A reference's current behavior may also differ from its gallery capture.

## Source, access, and reuse

Public browsing was available. The site has sponsorship and affiliate relationships, which should be considered when comparing promoted resources with editorial entries. Bookmarks are supported by local browser storage according to its policy. Neither browsing nor inspiration requires adding dependencies to our app. [Legal and privacy](https://minimal.gallery/legal/)

The legal policy claims rights in gallery screenshots, thumbnails, descriptions, metadata, and presentation. It permits limited personal viewing/bookmarking but restricts public or commercial reuse and bulk image redistribution without permission. The original websites have their own owners and rights; a gallery listing grants no source-code, logo, photo, or font license. A visible download control does not override the legal policy. Keep our documentation to original observations and direct links. [Reuse restrictions](https://minimal.gallery/legal/)

## Quality considerations and skill use

No DOM, contrast, axe-core, keyboard, or performance measurement was performed. Analytical concerns include very light secondary text, oversized type displacing essential content, and sparse layouts failing at high data density. Verify the implemented design at zoom, with long content, and on small screens; measure contrast instead of inferring it from a screenshot.

Skill role: art-direction research and reference provenance, especially for typography and disciplined composition. Recheck original sites before implementing behavior, gallery policies before reuse of any visual artifact, and acquisition or sponsorship changes if they affect curation or access.
