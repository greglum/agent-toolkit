## Purpose and useful evidence

[Shadcnblocks](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/) supplies React/shadcn/Tailwind blocks, components, themes, pages, and templates. The homepage includes operational categories such as application shells, dashboards, tables, and chart groups alongside a much larger marketing collection. Its size and “responsive” claims are vendor descriptions, not proof of task fit or accessibility.

The [Documentation index](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/docs) covers blocks, primitive families, CLI/MCP, Figma, templates, and CMS integrations. The [CLI guide](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/docs/shadcn-cli/overview) describes a namespaced registry and private-registry authentication. The [primitive overview](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/docs/primitive-libraries/overview) offers Radix UI, Base UI, and React Aria choices. Match the app's existing family instead of allowing an install to introduce incompatible wrappers.

## Patterns and application fit

For working apps, compare shells and tables by information hierarchy, available workspace, action placement, and responsive priority. A full-width marketing block answers a different problem from a dense records page. Preview content volume comparable to the actual app, including long labels, missing values, and permission-limited actions.

The strongest potential value is a coherent group of sections using familiar primitives. However, installing a whole admin kit can bring routes, theme assumptions, dependencies, and data examples beyond the requested task. Select a bounded structural idea and integrate it into the responsible local layer. Reuse existing components when they already own behavior.

Reference notes should record the selected block's job and why its structure is preferable. A large library is not a reason to replace a functioning interface wholesale. The decision must follow the user's task, real data, and the repository's architecture.

## Licensing, free access, and publication limits

The current [License](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/license) applies to paid licenses and permits end-product use with restrictions on separate component/derivative redistribution, competing libraries, templates, and marketplaces. Standard and Team plans have different permitted users. It explicitly delegates free-block/site access to the Terms; do not label the whole catalog MIT because shadcn/ui is MIT.

The [Terms](https://www.shadcnblocks.com/terms) allow free blocks in commercial/personal end products under specified conditions and distinguish this from publishing a component catalog. They limit automated collection, mirrors, training datasets, and platform redistribution, while permitting designated official-tool workflows and constrained directories. They also describe withdrawing some display permissions. Our page contains original product analysis and source links, with no component code, media, or catalog reproduction.

The homepage offers free/basic access with some login requirements and paid lifetime plans. Prices, counts, entitlements, and terms can change. Do not purchase, upload paid source to a public repo, or assume account access applies to every contributor. Check the exact item's applicable rights before adoption.
