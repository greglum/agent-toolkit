# Sources and departures

Observed October 30, 2026. All local prose and code are original. No upstream code, prompt,
fixture, media or license text was adapted.

- GitHub Lever contributors, [experiment arms](https://github.com/github/lever/tree/f859d0aa2be96981157ee0480655c84877e3cd98/skills/experiment-arms),
  revision `f859d0aa2be96981157ee0480655c84877e3cd98`, committed October 28, 2026,
  MIT. Inspected arm statistics, CI driver/metrics, adapter, find-the-loop and Loop Card.
  Useful mechanisms: unchanged controls, paired effects, visible failed attempts, a practical
  bar fixed before treatment and composed-change confirmation. Its CI collector is external
  writing machinery and is not included. Its analyzer's nominal intervals do not establish
  corrected many-arm evidence; unmatched A/A rounds can yield a zero reported floor.
- David Peter and hyperfine contributors, [README](https://github.com/sharkdp/hyperfine/blob/c5161524980522320c7d3425e714cc0965a869a5/README.md),
  revision `c5161524980522320c7d3425e714cc0965a869a5`, committed October 12, 2026,
  MIT or Apache-2.0. README and benchmark/output source excerpts describe repeated command
  measurement, warmup/preparation, raw exports and interference warnings. This tool does not
  replace hyperfine's collector or import its result format automatically.
- Victor Stinner and pyperf contributors, [analysis documentation](https://github.com/psf/pyperf/blob/00556e18777c36cfd96c91fdf1cd6bf70dd8c276/doc/analyze.rst)
  and [benchmark process](https://github.com/psf/pyperf/blob/00556e18777c36cfd96c91fdf1cd6bf70dd8c276/doc/run_benchmark.rst),
  revision `00556e18777c36cfd96c91fdf1cd6bf70dd8c276`, committed October 1, 2026,
  MIT. Also read the living 2.10.0 analysis/benchmark pages and selected comparison source.
  These support raw-value inspection, stability checks, explicit measurement conditions and
  a distinction between measurement processes and repeated values. The local tool does not
  implement pyperf's collector or inferential test.

The local analyzer checks supplied content and computes descriptive paired differences.
It deliberately omits sample-size guarantees, automatic outlier removal, p-values, bootstrap
intervals, causal claims, external collection, model routing and CI/account mutations.
A plan digest detects content changes when compared with a separately retained digest;
it cannot prove pre-registration time. Selection content and disjoint observation identities
support a checkable handoff but cannot prove fresh collection occurred after selection.
Recheck when these sources, schemas or measurement rules change, or a real trial exposes a
misleading decision. Catalog packaging and synthetic CLI tests do not establish skill benefit,
automatic selection or runtime performance of a measured product.

Round 2 independently checked NIST's [confirmation section](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section4/pri46.htm)
and [multiple-comparison section](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc47.htm),
observed October 30, 2026. Living-page reuse rights unassessed; no text copied. They support
fresh selected-setting confirmation in matched conditions and caution about repeated
uncorrected comparisons. Industrial sample-count advice is not a universal minimum here.
Independent review also exposed a preservation-binding omission: plan and sample identities
now include guard_contract, while the caller still owns truth and collection chronology.
