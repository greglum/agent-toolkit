# Craft source review

## October 23, 2026 craft sweep

[Timing defaults](timing.md), the script checks in [narration](narration.md#write-the-script),
the reference desk in [direction](direction.md#study-a-reference), the brief, gates, claim
check and critique loop in [production](production.md), and the motion notes in
[genres](genres.md#character-performance) and [Remotion](remotion.md) are original guidance. Numbers in them are
source defaults, paraphrased and not measured here; several sources call their own numbers
measured, which is unconfirmed. No upstream prose, code, media or presets were copied, and no
upstream script was run.

- [LottieFiles motion-design-skill](https://github.com/LottieFiles/motion-design-skill/tree/f9a8a041b85185ee4881b3471d3415e939aac772/skills/motion-design),
  MIT. Generalize eases by direction, exits shorter than entrances, stagger budgets, and the
  one-third moving rule. Motion personalities, emotion maps and ambient layers are UI scoped
  and not adopted.
- [Barty-Bart motion-graphics](https://github.com/Barty-Bart/motion-graphics/tree/3d83d987d658601d8a1269eb691622bdcd427335/skills),
  license NOASSERTION in the API, README says MIT. Generalize mix priority, duck depth,
  measuring loudness on the exported file, and sound placement by cue type. Its HUD look,
  synthesized music, rebuilt product UI and fixed feature budgets are not adopted.
- [howseen-ai claude-motion-design](https://github.com/howseen-ai/claude-motion-design/blob/3d90d349ef3fdde9b7e89de4df4a2159c9e8697f/skill/motion-design/SKILL.md),
  MIT. Generalize perceptual zoom, caption reading speed and duration, and a reviewer who
  must restate the film from frames. Its score gate, generated faces, remake mode and
  decorative effects are not adopted.
- [Remotion skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills/tree/6c67adcd9cdbe6eb84d76342da89b225f9161cb2/skills/remotion-best-practices),
  no license file found. Generalize the type floor and safe area scaled to frame width,
  perceptual scale, and narration-sized scenes. Its sound-effect list is not adopted.
- [HyperFrames public skill](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/tree/29643c7c8a3a4085f08f895d56c4b7cc072f2d69/skills/hyperframes),
  Apache-2.0. Generalize eases by role, reading holds, the still beat, narration pace, reveal
  on the spoken word, caption grouping, sound with a visible cause, the 360 px phone sheet,
  four-part review notes, and claims limited to what the source says. From the published
  skill we also take the moving-cut vector rule, the slow-fast-slow nudge and captions as an
  overlay; its partial-travel seam mandate is not adopted.
- [Mort1d motion-graphics-skills](https://github.com/Mort1d/motion-graphics-skills/tree/7f0c3714dc81bea52644a5287c6350345009e579/skills/motion-graphics),
  license NOASSERTION in the API, README says MIT. A single skill, not an index of others.
  Generalize one read at a time, hold floors, the hierarchy ratio, and measuring every encoded
  file's peak. Its constant camera push and promo pace targets are not adopted.
- [charlie947 motion-graphics-skills](https://github.com/charlie947/motion-graphics-skills/tree/229022ebca76972e119e36ada23a1648d51665ab),
  MIT, newer files only (vox explainer, animated chart, polish prompt). Generalize hedged
  claim wording and fixing one named thing at a time. Its look defaults and taste rules are
  not adopted.
- [Liamrjohnston remotion-motion-graphics-skill](https://github.com/Liamrjohnston/remotion-motion-graphics-skill/tree/b860c7921f2220b9e2a84e815ba0ae7b10858f08/skills),
  MIT. Generalize a critic separate from the builder. Its score gate is not adopted.
- [flyl1u fly-remotion](https://github.com/flyl1u/fly-remotion/blob/c5308fb028d78b7bf3b126d192c6153f66fceb91/skills/f-remotion/references/motion-library.md),
  MIT, motion library only, read in Chinese and paraphrased. Confirms one relationship per
  chart and screen-density limits; nothing else adopted.
- [Francesco, "Build Your Own AI Production Studio With Claude"](https://x.com/francescoinweb3/status/2108882188624121896),
  X article, October 10, 2026, observed October 23. No reuse license established. Concept
  discovery: a reference desk, finding the one true point before animating, and story before
  motion.
- [seeco, "Opus 5.5 Motion Design: What the Viral 'One-Prompt' Videos Are Hiding"](https://x.com/seeconvm/status/2106096193235259564),
  X article, October 2, 2026, observed October 23. No reuse license established. Concept
  discovery: the brief fields, six production gates, shots with entry and exit states, and
  grammar over subjects. Its 1 to 10 scoring loop, fixed cadence and banned-look lists are not
  acceptance rules.
- [beamnxw, "Motion Engineering with Opus 5.5: Build Launch Videos You Can Sell"](https://x.com/beamnxw/status/2108616740846420354),
  X article, October 9, 2026, observed October 23. No reuse license established. Concept
  discovery: one job per reference, 2 fps plus consecutive-frame sampling, measured, estimated
  and proposed labels, shots as events, and four-field corrections from an independent
  critic. Its constant subtle push is not adopted.
- Original script and claim rules, written October 23, 2026: open on the claim, one
  takeaway, plain words, single qualifiers, said/shown agreement, and a judgment pass after
  the scanners. Check claims against the primary record rather than a summary, since a
  summary can be wrong where the logs are right and a capture can contradict a spoken line.
  Lock independent competing drafts after that check.
